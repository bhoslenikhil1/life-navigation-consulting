Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What's the response time for queries?

We typically respond to WhatsApp messages within 2-4 hours on business hours. Emails are answered within 24 hours. Session bookings are confirmed within 1 hour.

❓ Can I reschedule a booked session?

Yes! You can reschedule up to 24 hours before your session. Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your new preferred time.

❓ What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, BHIM), WhatsApp Pay, bank transfers, and digital wallets. All payments are secured and encrypted.

❓ Is my information secure?

Absolutely. All your personal data and session information is encrypted and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties.

❓ Can I record my session?

Session recordings require explicit written consent. Please inform us beforehand if you wish to record. Without consent, recording is not permitted.

❓ What if I'm in crisis?

Our services are supportive counselling, not crisis intervention. If you're experiencing a mental health emergency, please contact:

Emergency: 112

❓ How do I book the masterclass?

Click "Masterclass Rs.99" on the home page. You'll be taken to the registration and payment page. Once payment is confirmed, you'll receive session details via email and WhatsApp.
