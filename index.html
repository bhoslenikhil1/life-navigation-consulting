Frequently Asked Questions

Is this professional therapy? +
No. Heal & Connect offers emotional wellness conversations. These sessions are supportive and complementary to therapy, but do not replace professional mental health treatment.
What happens during a session? +
We begin with a short grounding exercise, then create a calm space for you to share whatever feels heavy. You set the pace. There is no pressure to perform or “fix” anything.
Is everything confidential? +
Yes. Complete confidentiality is maintained. Your comfort and safety come first.
I feel very anxious about talking. Can I still join? +
Absolutely. Many people feel nervous at first. You are welcome exactly as you are — even if you speak very little.
