FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. Heal & Connect isn't therapy. It is an emotional support space where you express, release and reconnect with yourself. Therapy is clinical — this is human, conversational and grounding.

What happens in a session?

Your session begins with grounding, followed by a guided conversation that helps you express what feels heavy. You talk, reflect, pause, breathe — and walk out clearer than before.

Do I have to speak?

Yes — this is a two-way human experience. You don’t need to share deeply, but even small expression helps you feel lighter and connected.

Can I join if I feel anxious or lonely?

Absolutely. These sessions are designed especially for people dealing with emotional overload, loneliness, burnout, or unspoken feelings.

Is it confidential?

Yes. Everything stays between us. You speak freely — without fear of judgment or disclosure.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, you may reschedule once. Refunds are not available.

I have never spoken about my feelings before. Can I still come?

Yes — in fact, you are the person this space was created for. You can speak slowly, take pauses, or express only what feels comfortable.
