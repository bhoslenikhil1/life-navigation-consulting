FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. This is not clinical therapy. Heal & Connect offers guided emotional wellness circles — a safe, supportive space to talk, express, and feel heard. Many people find these sessions easier and more comfortable than traditional therapy, but it does not replace professional treatment.

Do I have to speak?

Yes — this is a two-way communication space. You don’t have to share everything, but speaking even a little helps you feel lighter, supported, and emotionally connected. You will never be forced, but gentle participation is encouraged.

Is everything confidential?

Absolutely. What you share stays strictly within the session. Heal & Connect follows a zero-judgment, zero-disclosure policy to ensure emotional safety for every participant.

What happens in a session?

The facilitator creates a calm space, introduces the topic, and guides the group through gentle sharing. You can express what you’re feeling, listen to others, and receive emotional grounding. The entire session is soft, calm, and structured.

Can I join without previous experience?

Yes. Most people join their first session feeling unsure — but within minutes, they feel comfortable. No preparation or prior experience is needed.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. You can reschedule your session once. Just contact us with your preferred new date and time. However, refunds are not available for missed sessions.
