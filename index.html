FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. Heal & Connect is not therapy. These are emotional wellness circles where you feel safe, heard and supported. It complements therapy, but doesn't replace it.

What happens in a session?

Your session begins with grounding, followed by a guided conversation that helps you express what feels heavy. You talk, reflect, pause, breathe — and walk out clearer than before.

Can I join if I feel anxious or lonely?

Absolutely. These sessions are designed especially for people dealing with emotional overload, loneliness, burnout, or unspoken feelings.

Is it confidential?

Yes, completely confidential with a zero-judgment policy.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, you may reschedule once. Refunds are not available.

I’ve never spoken about feelings before. Can I still come?

Yes — this space is created for exactly that. You can speak slowly, take pauses, and express only what feels comfortable.
