Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is this professional therapy?
+
No. Heal & Connect offers emotional wellness conversations. These sessions are supportive and complementary to therapy, but they do not replace professional mental health treatment. If you're in crisis, please reach out to a mental health professional.
What happens during a session?
+
We begin with a short grounding exercise to help you settle in, then create a calm space for you to share whatever feels heavy. You set the pace entirely. There is no pressure to perform, explain, or "fix" anything.
Is everything confidential?
+
Yes, completely. Your comfort and safety come first. Nothing you share will ever be recorded, stored, or disclosed to anyone. This space is entirely yours.
I feel anxious about talking. Can I still join?
+
Absolutely. Many people feel nervous at first, and that's completely okay. You are welcome exactly as you are — even if you speak very little, or need a long moment to settle. There's no right or wrong way to show up here.
What if I cry or get emotional?
+
Emotions are welcomed here — that's exactly why this space exists. Tears are not weakness; they're release. You will never be rushed or made to feel uncomfortable for feeling.
