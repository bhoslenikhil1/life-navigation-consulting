FAQ

Questions & Answers

Is this professional therapy or mental health treatment?
No. Speak & Settle provides structured counselling and analytical coaching, not clinical therapy or psychiatric treatment. Sessions are focused on practical guidance, decision-making support, and emotional clarity. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please consult a licensed mental health professional.
How does the session work — Chat or Call?
Sessions are conducted on WhatsApp — you may choose a text chat or a voice call, whichever you're more comfortable with. After payment confirmation, Nikhil will send you the session link or initiate contact at your scheduled time.
Is my information completely confidential?
Absolutely. Everything discussed in your session is strictly private. No recordings are made, no information is shared with any third party, and no notes are stored. Your privacy is a non-negotiable commitment.
What if I'm nervous or don't know where to start?
That's completely normal. Most people come in unsure of where to begin. Nikhil will guide the conversation from the start using structured questions that gently surface what matters most. You don't need to prepare anything — just show up.
Can I book multiple sessions?
Yes. Many clients find value in follow-up sessions as they implement advice and encounter new decisions. Each session is priced independently at Rs.399. Discuss your needs with Nikhil via WhatsApp and a schedule can be arranged.
What are the available session timings?
Sessions are available from 11 AM to 10 PM IST, 7 days a week. After payment, message Nikhil on WhatsApp with your preferred time and he will confirm availability within minutes.
