FAQ

Questions & Answers

Is this professional therapy or mental health treatment?
No. Speak & Settle provides structured counselling and analytical coaching, not clinical therapy. Sessions are focused on practical guidance and emotional clarity. If experiencing a mental health crisis, please consult a licensed mental health professional.
How does the session work — Chat or Call?
Sessions are on WhatsApp — text chat or voice call, whichever you prefer. After payment, Nikhil will initiate contact at your scheduled time.
Is my information completely confidential?
Absolutely. Everything discussed is strictly private. No recordings, no third-party sharing, no stored notes. Your privacy is a non-negotiable commitment.
What if I'm nervous or don't know where to start?
That's completely normal. Nikhil guides the conversation from the start using structured questions that gently surface what matters most. You don't need to prepare anything — just show up.
Can I book multiple sessions?
Yes. Many clients book follow-up sessions. Each is priced independently at Rs.399. Message Nikhil on WhatsApp to arrange a schedule.
What are the available session timings?
Sessions available 11 AM to 10 PM IST, 7 days a week. Message Nikhil on WhatsApp with your preferred time after payment.
